## VRS Status

The event is intended to meet Valve's Ranked Tier 2 requirements for tournaments without direct VRS invitations. Following our application, HLTV.org will determine which stages and matches count towards the VRS.

**VRS ranking status is not guaranteed to be confirmed before the event.** We are in direct contact with HLTV.org and will apply at the recommended time.

## Travel Requirements

All players must be able to enter Poland and participate in the LAN tournament. Teams are responsible for obtaining any required passports and visas.

The tournament operator may request proof that a team can attend the event. A team that cannot enter Poland may be excluded from any stage of the tournament.
